# Continuous A-share decision study

This is the owning integration contract for the user-approved 2026-09-05 expansion.
It replaces the four-case/two-ETF scope for new work; original registrations and
opened outcomes remain immutable historical evidence. `510300.SH` and `510500.SH`
are initial acceptance seeds. Research and dynamic admission may discover additional
A-share stocks or equity ETFs under the existing source-route and risk boundaries.

## Registration and claims

`continuous_study.py` freezes the original 15 cases plus three ordinary windows,
selected from prior-session features by content-hash ordering without deep-window
overlap. Direction, speed, volatility, cross-sectional dispersion, transitions and
information coverage remain separate dimensions. Price data cannot establish the
absence of news. Current constituents cannot establish historical industry membership.

`build_continuous_study_coverage_matrix` recomputes a separate evaluator-only
18-row matrix from that frozen registration and original dataset. It preserves raw
registered axes, capability targets, source support, and ordinary pre-cutoff price
features. Liquidity, dispersion, transition state, and news are explicit
`unknown_not_inferred` gaps; ordinary features are not converted into direction or
speed labels. `freeze_continuous_study_coverage_matrix` writes a separate
content-addressed `coverage-matrix.json` after reproducing the registration from its
pinned inputs. `report_continuous_study_coverage_matrix` verifies that artifact's
identity and evaluator-only boundary. These APIs do not change the frozen
registration, original coverage artifact, inventory, or preparation files.

All 18 cases retain initial research and portfolio diagnostics for Luna max, Terra
high and Sol high. Eight fixed deep windows produce 72 intended account paths:
eight cases, three models and three review schedules. Initial decisions are reused
within the same model/case through an explicit T0 adoption receipt, never by
fabricating native model calls or granting access to sibling continuations.
The long windows observe 120 trading sessions; forecasts still select 1/3/5/10/20/60.
The final observation cutoff constrains every new thesis. An intermediate update
does not have to shorten its forecast merely because the preceding thesis has aged.

The control reviews at thesis expiry; the scheduled arm also uses the selected
horizon's registered offsets. The event arm allows at most three intermediate
reviews before an expiry-originated review replenishes its allowance. Changing an
intermediate thesis does not reset that allowance. Fact identities remain consumed
across renewals, and simultaneous reasons share a cutoff/input decision identity.
H1 can make schedules coincide; that is a result, not an input error.
The daily input entry uses qualified source-record identities and an absolute 3%
completed-session adjusted-price move as its frozen event threshold. Changing a
context envelope's timestamp alone does not create another fact.

## Data and decision boundaries

`OnDemandResearch` exposes Harness-bound semantic queries to the real pi read-only
tool loop. A miss records an acquisition request. `analyze_with_acquisition` seals
the old Run incomplete before acquiring data, persists the response and snapshot,
then starts a bound successor Run. Prospective receipt advances the cutoff. An
explicit Decision Episode keeps its deadline across successors and shares the
global budget; a new Run never replenishes money, request or event allowances.
Historical live-search misses remain typed gaps until a permitted historical source
projection is available. Past model opinions and Recall summaries are not source facts.

The production `agent prospective-discovery` entry reopens actual parent acquisition
receipts and freezes the latest 24 news records by publication time and observation
identity. Raw records remain in CAS; compact model documents retain their exact
source record and proof. Each model has its own discovery episode and may select a
new company or ETF through an actual native profile query. Confirmed identity causes
outside-Run admission-data acquisition and a candidate-targeted successor thesis.
Dynamic admission then either supplies an eligible candidate to an existing portfolio
authority or reports exact refusal gaps. The current CLI has no accepted current
Mock account or prospective trading-rule authority; a research-context identifier is
not an account snapshot. This entry therefore cannot claim completed Mock acceptance.

The existing authority store owns content-addressed raw responses, versioned
observations, coverage and acquisition transactions. Durable claims deduplicate
across processes. Failed parsing preserves bounded secret-free raw responses for
offline repair. Raw daily range reuse only fetches uncovered intervals under the
same source/configuration/rights policy. Source corrections retain earlier receipts.

`HistoricalAShareInputs` reopens source CAS and effective trading rules.
`research_series` is independent of trading eligibility: it filters completed
sessions and adjusts research prices using factors available through that cutoff.
It neither creates cash dividends from factors nor makes those prices executable.
Current input objects reuse verified immutable source tables; new snapshot bindings
reopen the source graph. `continuous_research_repository` carries old qualified
context at its original time and labels sparse news coverage. It excludes current
industry constituents and case labels from model inputs.

`ContinuousPortfolioRuntime` reads the authoritative account prefix, signed current
thesis and scoped Recall. Research can complete when tradability is missing;
portfolio execution then remains incomplete with the exact gap. Dynamic admission
creates a new immutable concrete Universe/Mandate. It cannot grant old orders new
authority. A BUY must also fit cutoff-known upper-limit notional and source-backed
fees, settled cash, exposure and turnover limits without rewriting the model's
signed deterministic quantity.

Each arm owns one `HistoricalStreamingAccount` using the pinned Nautilus engine.
The CNY 100,000 starting allocation is approximately half overnight 510300 and cash,
with the same bootstrap transaction/fees in every comparable path. Daily raw
execution, valuation, risk, T+1, fills/no-fills and reconciliation retain their
original identities on restart. Supported source-backed cash distributions use
record-date inventory; conflicting effects or unsupported corporate actions block
affected execution. Missing account authority, system errors and exhausted budget
are incomplete work, not `hold`.

V5 rotation initially admits only a full source close. A fresh reconciled account
and another model review are required for the destination; partial fills, unknown
acknowledgments or T+1 restrictions do not release it. The later review may retain
cash. Every leg has separate identity, risk approval and recovery evidence.

## Budget, measurements and delivery

The shared machine/project study owner freezes USD 40 cumulatively: route 1,
analysis 9, portfolio 2.5, rolling 22, unseen/prospective 2.5 and recovery 3.
Reconciled prior usage is 98 requests, USD 5.356905 known and USD 0.011769 reserved
for one unknown request. Old usage is charged once, not reset by another root.
No new paid data service or real broker execution is authorized by this study.

Data/source and baseline preflight precede paid batches. Input-size, model pricing,
output reserves and review assumptions are frozen; uncertain future review counts
are reported separately. The actual shared budget remains the hard dispatch gate.
Partial batches retain the fixed 72-path denominator and complete registered
calendars, advancing across market states rather than spending first on one regime.
Daily frontier checkpoints report `prefix_complete` only as progress. Replay still
reopens every signed decision and its exact engine input; a populated NAV file does
not make a failed decision validation acceptable. Such curves remain diagnostic and
cannot produce measured strategy comparisons. Research coverage gaps are included in
every result row independently of completed model judgments.

Measurements use NAV paths after execution fees: compounded return, drawdown,
empirical daily-return CVaR, cash, turnover, no-fills, residual positions and model
cost. Cadence pairs must share initial economics, execution policy and every date.
Avoided loss is capped by the control's loss; outperforming a losing control is not
all loss avoidance. Causal correction requires reopened thesis/trigger evidence.
Small-sample tail statistics remain descriptive. USD model costs are reported
separately from CNY NAV unless an explicit FX accounting rule is registered.

The opt-in `qualified_seed_etf_exchange_rule_v1` scenario binds dated rule and
issuer evidence and explicitly models normal-session ETF limits. It preserves
reported-limit disagreements as diagnostics and never upgrades current receipts to
strict historical PIT. Corporate-action reference changes, unsupported special
sessions and missing required execution inputs remain blocking. The default
reported-limit scenario and its previous evidence remain unchanged.

For this qualified scenario, source gates and matched executable baselines use the
registered deep observation horizon (including 120-session slow-bull and bear
windows). The full original calendar remains bound as a separate diagnostic, rather
than extending that matched horizon. Required initial 510300 holdings and complete
matched baselines gate a case; optional candidate gaps are retained separately. A
candidate still needs day-specific admission before an order, and a held candidate
requires valid daily execution inputs. Qualified matched baselines have their own
journal namespace so full-path and matched-path evidence cannot collide.

Matched cash, unchanged initial account and broad ETF baselines use the same engine
and fees. Full raw index paths are separately labeled non-executable diagnostics.
The fourth executable baseline gives the existing Phase 2 four-adjusted-close
momentum calculation a new explicit continuous 510300 binding: positive direction
targets the maximum affordable long position; flat or negative direction targets
cash. Sizing uses pre-open evidence, and the four closes must be consecutive source
calendar sessions. This does not rewrite the original Phase 2 experiment.
No existing executable volatility policy was found; volatility-based window
selection is not a trading rule. Fixed and lagged industry index baselines also lack
historical tradable mappings and remain explicit executable-baseline gaps.

Development, unseen validation, prospective evidence, local Mock acceptance and
IBKR Paper acceptance are separate reports. The `ibkr-paper prepare` entry only
writes an immutable offline checklist/fault matrix and always reports execution
unaccepted. Actual new receipts and naturally due reviews are required for prospective
acceptance; test fixtures cannot be promoted into natural events or broker orders.
