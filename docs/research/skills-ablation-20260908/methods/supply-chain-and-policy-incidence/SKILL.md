# supply-chain-and-policy-incidence

Experimental research method; frozen research cutoff: 2021-09-07.

Evidence status: Conditional transmission framework; sector timing and capital-expenditure cycle rules remain hypotheses.

Question: Who receives a price or demand benefit, who bears added costs, and how can competitors or inventories change it?
Evidence: physical supply/demand and inventory where available; input/output exposures; contracts, pass-through and hedges; capacity/capital-expenditure plans; regulation text and effective date; currency and export exposure.
Steps: distinguish supply from demand shocks; map both sides of the income statement; compare output protection with input costs and retaliation; consider inventories and delayed competitive capacity. Sum comparable exposures only; otherwise state direction and uncertainty. A commodity decline can help buyers while signaling weaker end demand.
Action conditions: a long candidate needs a plausible net benefit at the observed price, not merely a favorable industry headline. Existing holdings may be retained despite a negative input shock if offsetting facts are stronger. Compare likely magnitude, duration and what prices already imply.
Failure conditions: poor pass-through, hedged exposure, demand destruction, retaliation, substitution, inventory losses or new capacity eroding margins. Macroeconomic welfare is not a stock-return forecast.
Exit and re-entry: review at inventory, order, margin or capacity milestones; exit an invalidated net-benefit thesis and reconsider when exposures or price change. Avoid anchoring re-entry to the old commodity price.
Supporting case: 2014-15 oil analysis distinguishes supply-driven relief for users from demand weakness. Counter-case: 2018-19 US tariffs offered output protection while imported-input costs and foreign retaliation offset benefits in manufacturing evidence.
Transfer limits: the legal tariff scope, supply chain, pricing power and exchange rate must be matched to each A-share firm. No validated semiconductor/inventory timing rule or Chinese sector return claim is supplied.
