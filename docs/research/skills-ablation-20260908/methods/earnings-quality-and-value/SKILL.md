# earnings-quality-and-value

Experimental research method; frozen research cutoff: 2021-09-07.

Evidence status: Cross-sample research motivates a framework; no standalone A-share alpha or specific issuer case validated.

Question: Is a favorable result incremental, sustainable and available at a price that compensates for risk?
Evidence: reported revenue/profit/cash flow and publication dates; comparable periods and one-off items; a timestamped prior expectation or explicit absence; leverage, refinancing, dilution and valuation; pre-announcement price movement.
Steps: distinguish a high earnings level, a positive surprise and an investable return. Compare profitability to the capital/assets required. Reconcile cash conversion and working capital where data exist. Test whether low valuation reflects temporary disappointment or deteriorating business value. Keep accounting period, announcement date and retrieval time separate.
Action conditions: consider long exposure when sustainable cash-flow prospects and the price offer a defensible case against alternatives. Hold or reduce if revisions, quality or price weaken it; missing consensus means the surprise is unknown, not that all analysis must stop.
Failure conditions: stale statements, incomparable accounting, one-offs, receivables growth, financing stress, dilution or an already expensive quality premium. Do not invent a forward estimate from trailing ratios.
Exit and re-entry: tie review to the next report, cash conversion, guidance, funding milestone or valuation change. Re-entry can follow repaired economics and an acceptable price even if the stock rose after exit.
Supporting evidence: gross profitability and quality research finds historically informative relationships across portfolios and markets. Counter-explanation: announcement-related attention and trading volume can generate price moves without proving improved cash flows; quality also has a price. A cheap multiple alone does not resolve distress.
Transfer limits: the cited factor portfolios often include shorts and older samples. This card has no independently verified 2006-2021 issuer-level value-trap case; that is a recorded evidence gap, not a claimed successful case study.

Sources (publication dates, not retrieval dates):
- 2007-05: [Lamont and Frazzini: Earnings Announcement Premium and Trading Volume](https://www.nber.org/papers/w13090).
- 2010-04: [Novy-Marx: The Other Side of Value](https://www.nber.org/papers/w15940).
- 2018-11-05: [Asness, Frazzini and Pedersen: Quality Minus Junk](https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Research/Working-Paper/Quality-Minus-Junk).
