## Evidence and claims

The engine is pinned to NautilusTrader 1.231.0. Its existing adapter uses
`run(streaming=True)` across sessions, then `end()` and `dispose()` at close;
acceptance must exercise this adapter, not a test accounting substitute. The
[upstream lifecycle](https://nautilustrader.io/docs/latest/concepts/backtesting/apis-and-runs/)
and [LEAN responsibility split](https://www.quantconnect.com/docs/v2/writing-algorithms/algorithm-framework/portfolio-construction/key-concepts)
are references, not additional state owners. Paired opportunities control known
conditions in the sense of [blocked designs](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pri/section3/pri332.htm);
two inspected windows remain development evidence, not investment promotion.

Each batch is independently reviewed. Final evidence distinguishes implemented,
offline accepted, missing source authority, prepared, and awaiting real-model or
natural-event validation. Passing engineering tests does not establish investment
value or real-model interface usability.

## Authorized staged follow-through after the first account contrast

The user subsequently authorized progressing the proposed optimization sequence until
an actual budget, research-scope, product, or broker decision requires their input.
The two outcomes remain independent: trustworthy Agent-directed automation and
incremental investment performance after execution costs, with risk reported alongside
return. This extends local research/preparation work; it does not reuse old paid
allowances, alter frozen studies, promote a strategy, or grant broker mutation authority.

The immediate sequence is:

1. Reopen existing complete account evidence and attribute the result to the
   pre-executable gap, post-executable exposure, and fees. Add an explicitly posthoc
   deterministic exposure comparison with the same initial account and market
   conditions. Reconcile every component to authoritative final NAV; preserve old
   paid failures and keep additional model calls at zero.
2. Verify the next study's source and predecessor readiness before preparing paid
   dispatch. Source qualification must not depend on realized outcomes. Keep the
   frozen energy five-Judgment program distinct from any proposed single-model H5
   successor. Missing source facts or rejected candidates remain visible.
3. Only after comparable event-evidence results and source gates pass, prepare the
   target-selection study, then continuous-review study under their existing gates.
   A one-shot saved-response contrast cannot be relabeled continuous review.
4. Address demonstrated interface friction with existing parser/replay owners;
   unknown authority, PIT, identity and evidence violations stay closed. Preserve
   and reuse completed fixes rather than repeat broad refactors or tests.
5. Prepare the existing Paper acceptance scenarios separately. Any actual broker
   mutation still needs the exact authorized scope and approval required by the
   owning execution boundary.

Advance on complete, interpretable evidence. Stop the relevant paid step for missing
inputs, recurring contract failure, absent complete-pair capacity, or exhausted
spending authority. A few development events can decide whether to continue but do
not replace the formal acceptance program. Material protocol changes and a new
spending allocation must be presented with a concrete prepared proposal, expected
learning, maximum cost and explicit failure accounting before asking the user.
