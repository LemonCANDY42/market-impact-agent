# Adversarial Countercase and Risk

Use this general method to obtain the useful work of bull, bear, and risk-review roles without
running a debate or assigning the Agent a preferred conclusion.

1. Construct the strongest evidence-supported case for the proposed impact and the strongest
   evidence-supported countercase.
2. For each side, distinguish facts, assumptions, missing facts, and causal claims.
3. Test alternative explanations, offsetting mechanisms, timing mismatch, crowded positioning,
   market-regime dominance, liquidity, and data-quality failure when relevant evidence exists.
4. Do not manufacture symmetry: one side may have no support, but that absence must follow from
   the evidence rather than the assigned role.
5. State observable invalidation conditions and the earliest evidence that would distinguish
   the competing cases.
6. Reduce confidence or abstain when the countercase breaks a critical transmission link.
