# Public-Equity Exposure Analysis

Use this general method when an event may transmit to listed companies. It combines the stable
parts of fundamental, sector, and target-mapping analysis without assuming that a company is a
beneficiary or casualty.

1. Start from the frozen eligible universe and pre-outcome Exposure Registry.
2. Map the event to observable operating variables: volume, realized price, input cost,
   capacity, utilization, inventory, working capital, financing, or policy access.
3. Continue through industry economics to the listed target, recording each causal hand-off
   and its directness. Correlation or a shared sector label is not a transmission step.
4. Separate producer, processor, transporter, distributor, and end-user economics. Test
   substitution, hedging, regulated prices, currency, contract terms, and geographic mismatch.
5. Use revenue shares, margins, balance-sheet facts, valuation multiples, or analyst revisions
   only when cited evidence supplies them. Otherwise state the missing exposure proof.
6. Compare every proposed target with eligible alternatives and controls before selecting one.
