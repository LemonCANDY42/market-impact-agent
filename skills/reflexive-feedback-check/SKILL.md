# Reflexive Feedback Check

Use this method only when frozen evidence may support a self-reinforcing or self-defeating loop
between beliefs, market behavior, and operating or financing facts.

1. Start from the non-market fact and the prevailing belief separately.
2. Trace a complete loop: fact -> belief or expectation -> allocation, leverage, collateral,
   financing, capacity, or forced flow -> changed operating or financing fact. Cite every link.
3. Distinguish a reinforcing phase, a test or reversal point, and a merely observed price trend.
   If price cannot alter a relevant fact, record that the reflexive loop is unproven.
4. Test a conventional exogenous explanation and a liquidity-only explanation before using the loop.
5. State the observable break conditions and abstain when a link is inferred from price alone.

Do not impersonate George Soros or any investor. Do not call every rally a bubble, every decline a
reflexive collapse, or predict the magnitude, duration, or turning point of a feedback process.
