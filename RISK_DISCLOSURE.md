# Risk Disclosure

Market Impact Agent is experimental software for research and engineering. It
does not provide financial, investment, legal, tax, or fiduciary advice and does
not promise profitability, accuracy, risk-adjusted returns, or continuous
availability.

News and market data may be late, incomplete, incorrect, revised, or unavailable.
Models can hallucinate, overfit historical events, misunderstand expectations,
and fail during regime changes. Backtests and paper trading cannot reproduce
all liquidity, queue priority, spread, slippage, halts, limits, settlement,
corporate actions, broker outages, or operational failures.

Real trading can lose more than expected and may be irreversible. No live path
should be enabled without independent testing, explicit authorization, hard
limits, reconciliation, monitoring, and a kill switch. The user remains solely
responsible for any decision to connect the software to a broker or use real
capital.
